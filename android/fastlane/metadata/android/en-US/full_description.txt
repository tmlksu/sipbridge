SIP Bridge is the Android client for <b>sipbridge</b>, a self-hosted setup that lets
you use your home SIP extension from outside the house <b>without opening any port on
your router</b> and <b>without listening on plaintext UDP 5060</b> on the phone.

<b>You must run the relay yourself.</b> This app is useless on its own — it talks to
<i>sipbridge relay</i>, a small Go service (Docker image provided) that you run on your
own LAN next to your PBX. The relay registers to your SIP server as an ordinary
extension and exposes a single authenticated WebSocket to the app. Any SIP registrar
works: Asterisk, FreePBX, or the built-in SIP server of a Japanese fibre-optic
telephony home gateway (NTT HGW).

<b>How it works</b>

App --WSS (JSON + RTP frames)--> your reverse proxy / tunnel --> relay --SIP+RTP on
your LAN--> your PBX

The phone opens no listening socket at all. Signalling and audio share one outbound
TLS WebSocket, so the connection works from behind CGNAT and hotel Wi-Fi alike.

<b>Features</b>

* Incoming and outgoing calls, G.711 (PCMU/PCMA)
* Keypad, call history, optional on-device contact name lookup
* Full-screen incoming call over the lock screen
* Persistent mode: a foreground service holds the WebSocket open (good for an
  always-powered tablet or kiosk device)
* Multiple SIP accounts, configured from the app and stored by the relay
* Settings encrypted at rest (EncryptedSharedPreferences)

<b>About this build</b>

This is the <i>foss</i> flavour. It contains no Google Play Services and no Firebase,
so <b>push notifications are not available</b> — the app must stay connected in
persistent mode to receive calls. That is the intended setup for a device that is
always powered on.

<b>Privacy</b>

The app talks only to the relay URL you configure. There is no analytics, no
telemetry, and no third-party service. Audio is carried inside the TLS WebSocket to
your own relay; note that if you terminate TLS at a CDN or tunnel provider, that
provider can see the media. Contact names, when enabled, are resolved entirely
on-device and never leave the phone.

Setup instructions, the relay source and the protocol specification are in the source
repository.
